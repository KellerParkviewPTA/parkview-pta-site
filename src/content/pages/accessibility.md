Parkview Elementary PTA is committed to making this website usable by everyone, including people with disabilities. We aim to meet WCAG 2.1 Level AA standards, including:

- Clear, high-contrast color combinations
- Full keyboard navigation support
- Descriptive alt text on meaningful images
- Visible focus indicators
