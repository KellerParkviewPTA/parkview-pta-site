title: "Membership Info"
---
Your membership helps fund everything the PTA does for Parkview students and teachers, from classroom grants to community events. Membership is open to parents, guardians, teachers, staff, grandparents, and community members alike.

**No meetings required.** You can be as involved as you want to be. Show up to everything, or simply join to support our school. Either way, you're making a difference.
