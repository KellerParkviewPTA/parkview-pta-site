Parkview Elementary PTA has been proudly serving our Keller ISD community for years. What started as a small group of parents organizing a single fall carnival has grown into a full partnership that supports classrooms, funds field trips, organizes teacher appreciation events, and brings our whole school community together throughout the year.

We're an all-volunteer board, and we'd love for you to join us. No meetings required to be a member!
