### Keller ISD Background Check Requirement

Yes, Keller ISD requires a background check for volunteers. Anyone given an assignment or task dealing directly with students other than their own, such as field trips, class parties, Field Day, or Watchdogs, needs a current background check on file. This is an annual requirement, so it must be completed every school year, even if you volunteered the year before.

To apply, complete the Keller ISD volunteer application. Processing takes about 10 business days, so apply early and don't wait until right before your volunteer commitment.

[Complete the Keller ISD Volunteer Application](https://kellerisd.schoolspring.com/?jobid=5769807)

If you run into login trouble, call the PowerSchool Applicant Help line at 1-877-974-7437.
